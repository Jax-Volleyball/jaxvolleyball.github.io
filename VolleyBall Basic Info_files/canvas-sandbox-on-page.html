All about volleyball!

Volleyball is a fast team sport where players work together to send the ball over a net. It is fun, active and played by millions of people around the world.

See the basic rules

VOLLEYBALL

Basic rules

Here are some important rules to remember when playing.

Each team can touch the ball up to three times before sending it back.

The ball must go over the net and land inside the other team’s court.

A team scores a point whenever the ball hits the floor on the other side.

Players rotate positions each time their team wins back the serve.

Volleyball positions

Every player has a job to help the team. Here are six common positions.

Setter

Sets up the ball for attackers.

Outside hitter

Attacks from the left side.

Middle blocker

Blocks attacks near the net.

Opposite hitter

Attacks from the right side.

Libero

A defensive player who digs the ball.

Defensive specialist

Helps with passing and defence.

Equipment used

Volleyball — light and soft enough to pass, set and spike.

Net — separates the two teams and is set at the correct height.

Court shoes — help players stop, jump and move safely.

Interesting volleyball facts

Volleyball was invented in 1895 in the United States.

Indoor volleyball teams have six players on the court at a time.

Beach volleyball is usually played with two players on each team.

A quick history of volleyball

Volleyball has been around for more than 100 years. Here are a few important moments.

1895

William G. Morgan invented volleyball in Massachusetts, USA.

1964

Indoor volleyball first appeared at the Olympic Games in Tokyo.

1996

Beach volleyball joined the Olympics at the Atlanta Games.

Cool facts about famous players

These players have made a big mark on the sport.

Karch Kiraly

He is the only person to win Olympic gold in both indoor volleyball and beach volleyball.

Lang Ping

She won Olympic medals as a player for China and later coached China to Olympic gold.

Giba

The Brazilian star helped his team win Olympic gold and multiple world titles.
